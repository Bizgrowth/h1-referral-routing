How a referral moves through it

Everything runs on the client's own computer. Nothing is assigned without a person.

Read the referral export
The spreadsheet the team already uses. No new system to fill in.
Spreadsheet in

Keep only approved fields
11 allowed columns. Patient, claim and contact columns are dropped; SSN, phone, email and ZIP values blanked.

3
Flag missing or invalid details

Caught before routing
4

5
Coordinator decides
Approve, change vendor or reject. Each decision is logged with the time it took.

Missing-info rate, shortlist accuracy, review time, and the baseline. Never individual records.
